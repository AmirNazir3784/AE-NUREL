## Evidence-based editorial decisions

The directory's visible article links are dominated by named-editor instructions,
product comparisons and caption-delivery questions. It also contains press and
business articles. This observation establishes topic coverage, not demand,
ranking, quality or traffic. The workbook's many video-to-MP3 query variants are
not evidence that all of them belong in this caption-focused batch.

Retain the specific repair tutorials: malformed SRT, encoding, overlap, split
origin and merger offsets solve different concrete problems and connect to real
AENural tools. They need not be discarded because CaptionX does not feature the
same repair title in its directory.

Refocus two broad comparison pages without adding posts or changing their URLs:

- **Free Auto Caption Generator: Check Exports Before Editing** replaces the
  broader software-choice framing. Its opening answers the locked-download
  problem; its trial/export steps and recovery advice come before a long edit.
- **SRT Translator Problems: Check Meaning, Timing and Export** replaces the
  broad translator-choice framing. Its diagnostic table maps changed meaning,
  inconsistent names, missing cues, fast text and failed downloads to distinct
  checks and actions. Existing exact AENural inspection steps remain.

The other 48 tasks remain distinct. Do not replace specific troubleshooting with
unresearched “best tool” lists just to reproduce a competitor's title pattern.
There are still workbook intents requiring separate research; see the complete
[coverage report](keyword-coverage-2026-11-12.md).

## Claims that must not be copied

There are inconsistent access claims within the observed pages. The software
comparison describes an account-free, watermark-free video output, whereas the
CapCut tutorial describes account-required, watermarked free video export. The
translation comparison's account-related heading and account-related body also
differ. These are observable text discrepancies, not a test of actual entitlements.

Use the original vendor documentation and the real account/output being tested
before publishing price, access, language, version or export claims. Do not import
unverified accuracy percentages, speed comparisons, engagement promises or
universal reading limits. No AENural superiority benchmark is claimed.

Useful patterns to adapt originally: answer the task early, name the required
deliverable, give exact supported steps, distinguish platform/edition limits,
show a relevant example, and link a genuinely available next action. Preserve
AENural's design and existing article-specific original diagrams. No competitor
text, images, code or proprietary assets were copied.
