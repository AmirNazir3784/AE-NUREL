# How to Convert Audio to Text Online for Free (MP3, WAV & Voice Recordings)

A free audio to text converter online turns interviews, lectures, and voice notes into editable text. Upload a supported audio recording, run transcription, review the result, and download it. This guide covers practical steps, file formats, free limits, and common mistakes.

## How to Convert Audio to Text Online for Free

1. **Choose a transcription tool.** Find an audio-to-text converter that accepts existing audio files, not just live dictation. Check supported languages, upload limits, and whether downloads cost extra.
2. **Upload your recording.** Select an MP3, WAV, or saved voice memo. If uploading fails, confirm the actual file format and size instead of changing its filename extension.
3. **Set the spoken language.** Choose the recording's language when possible. Automatic detection is convenient but can struggle with short clips or conversations switching languages.
4. **Start transcription and review the text.** Speech recognition produces a draft transcript. Replay unclear passages; correct names, dates, technical terms, and speaker changes. Never rely on an unchecked transcript for exact quotations. If available, switch on speaker labels for interviews with several participants.
5. **Copy or download your transcript.** Choose TXT for notes. For subtitles, look for timestamped SRT or VTT exports. Not every free service offers these formats.

For example, a student could upload a recorded lesson, correct unfamiliar subject terms, and save searchable notes for revision. Keep the original audio for reference.

## Convert MP3, WAV, and Phone Voice Recordings

### MP3 to Text

MP3 works well for interviews, classes, and podcasts. Smaller files can upload faster, although excessive compression may make speech harder to recognize.

### WAV to Text

Uncompressed PCM WAV recordings avoid lossy compression but can be larger. Check file-size restrictions before uploading audio.

### iPhone and Android Voice Recordings

On iPhone, Voice Memos recordings can be shared as M4A files. Android formats vary between recording apps. Save or export the recording, then use a converter that accepts its format.

## How to Choose a Truly Free Audio to Text Converter

Compare what “free” includes. Some tools transcribe short recordings without registration; others require accounts or restrict free minutes. Check file sizes, languages, and whether TXT or subtitle exports are included.

Read the privacy policy before sharing a recording. Check how the service handles your files, how long it retains them, and how you can delete them.

Google Docs voice typing is designed for microphone dictation, not uploading an MP3. For saved recordings, choose a file-transcription tool. Check cancellation terms before paying for an upgrade.

## Improve Accuracy and Fix Common Problems

Record away from background noise, position the microphone near speakers, and avoid overlapping conversation. If transcription selects the wrong language, change it manually and retry.

For failed uploads, check file size, recording length, and supported formats. Split long recordings when necessary.

Before submitting private interviews or client conversations, obtain required permission and read retention policies. Always verify numbers, names, and quotations against the audio.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I convert MP3 audio to text online for free?

Yes. Some services transcribe MP3 files at no charge, although usage limits vary.

### Can I transcribe audio without registering?

Yes, some tools allow guest transcription. Check their download restrictions first.

### Can I convert iPhone voice recordings into text?

Yes. Export Voice Memos as M4A and choose a compatible transcription tool.

### Is free audio transcription unlimited?

Not necessarily. Free plans often restrict minutes, uploads, or file sizes.

### Why does my transcript contain mistakes?

Noise, overlapping voices, names, or incorrect language detection can cause errors. Review the original recording.
