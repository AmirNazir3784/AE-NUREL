Formats, processing and privacy

English speech recognition runs on your computer using Whisper Small. The first use downloads public model files (about 250 MB of weights, plus runtime assets); no audio is sent to a transcription server. MP3/WAV decoding depends on your browser. Model loading can take time and accuracy varies with sound quality. You can import SRT/VTT without loading an AI model, manually correct captions, and export files even when offline after the page has loaded.
